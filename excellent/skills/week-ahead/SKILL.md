# Week ahead

Read only; change nothing.

1. Call `overview` for the group's venues, upcoming events, new inquiries and outstanding balance.
2. Call `agenda` (7 days from today, or the window the person names; one venue with `location`). Go day by
   day: each booking with its time, rooms, host and balance; holds that lapse this week (a decision is due);
   payments owed or overdue; and inquiries nobody has answered. Lead with the suggestions it ranks, most urgent
   first, each with its link into Excellent.
3. Call `report` with `period` month (or quarter) for revenue booked, collected and outstanding, the pace
   against last period and the goal, and the win rate. Use the plain on-track verdict it gives.
4. Call `schedule_week` and name the stretches of opening hours nobody is scheduled for, open shifts, and
   whether each venue's week is still a draft staff can't see.

Amounts come back in cents: show them as dollars. If the figures say they count sample (demo) records, say so.
Keep it to one phone screen, then offer the next step: answer an inquiry, staff the gaps, or invoice an event.

Safety: this skill only reads. Nothing is drafted, changed, sent, charged or deleted.
